# Chapter Two — The Drowned Meridian

Unlocked after **What the ferryman saw**, the final conversation of Chapter One. Speak to Tobin again at the west jetty to begin. Existing completed saves unlock the new chapter automatically.

The Moonwell's light has exposed three locks imprisoning the Tidewarden's voice. Tobin returns Maren's keeper book and reveals the spells for the sleeping portals. The journey follows three maps in order: **City → Pine Islet → Skibidi Yard → City**, then returns to the Moonwell for its ending.

| Mission | Map | Challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A chart that bleeds | City, west jetty | Hear Tobin's warning about the moving tide chart and receive the keeper's portal spells. |
| The memory beneath the roots | Pine Islet, through the city portal | Read the book and cast the passage spell. Read the tablet and activate three runes in its remembered order. A mistake resets the sequence. |
| Bells for a missing dawn | Skibidi Yard, through Pine Islet's portal | Opening the root lock reveals the next book spell. Follow a drowned bell-ringer's verse to ring three scattered bells in order. |
| Forty-five borrowed seconds | Skibidi Yard, near the landing | Close three pressure valves within 45 seconds. The first valve starts the timer. |
| Keep the last light burning | Skibidi Yard, meridian beacon | Defeat three waves of 2, 3, and 4 drowned echoes. Each has 140 health and hits harder than Chapter One's ghosts. |
| The voice beneath the tide | Skibidi Yard, beyond the stacks | Defeat the 600-health Hollow Warden. Escape or jump its red pulse; attack during the silver shield opening. |
| A promise with no keeper | City, Moonwell Sanctuary | Freeing the Tidewarden's voice reveals the homeward book spell. Return through the yard's portal and bring the voice to the seal. |

Every crossing begins at a physical book beside the portal. The hero reads the incantation and then casts it to awaken the gate; ordinary attacks and abilities cannot activate it. The first passage requires the keeper's ledger, the restored Moonwell, Tobin's farewell, and his Chapter Two chart conversation. Pine Islet's passage waits for the root lock, and the yard's homeward passage waits for all three locks, the vigil, and the Hollow Warden. After the ending, the three portals remain available for exploration in the same circuit.

The destination starts downloading while the hero reads and the portal awakens. Travel waits until the destination is ready. The runtime releases the previous map's scene and graphics resources after the crossing to save memory; map files are not deleted from disk. The book interaction can be tried again if the download fails.

Use **F** (or the on-screen interaction button) at the new objects, **Q/click** to attack, **E** for the special ability, and **Space** to jump. The gold map diamond points to the current mission; blue rings mark its interactive objects. The tracker shows sequence progress, valve time, wave counts, and boss health/shield state. The journal keeps the Chapter One recap and the route between maps.

The valve panel is a clue, not a timer switch. If pressure returns, retry from any valve. Falling or leaving a combat arena resets that attempt. Completed missions and rewards are saved immediately; unfinished sequences, valves and combat attempts restart on reload. Menus and conversations pause challenge timers.

<details>
<summary>Puzzle solutions and boss timing</summary>

- Runes: **root → rain → moon**.
- Bells: **dusk → tide → dawn**.
- Valves can be closed in any order.
- The Hollow Warden warns for **3.4 seconds**, then pulses and opens its shield for **3.6 seconds**. Its pulse reaches 10 world units. A jump above one unit at impact also avoids damage.
- Leaving more than 42 world units from a combat arena resets it.
