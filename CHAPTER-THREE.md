# Chapter Three — The Shared Flame

Begins at the **meridian seal beside the Moonwell** after completing Chapter Two. Existing saves keep both earlier chapters and unlock this continuation automatically.

The restored Tidewarden asks the Reach to share its watch. A recovered flame reveals the **Ashen Observatory**, an original island of broken astronomical instruments, memorials and a central star chamber. Meet **Ilyra**, the astronomer who hid the shore from the Long Tide, and **Oren**, the apprentice who volunteered to be its last witness. Discover why the island vanished, then confront **the Unwritten**, which feeds on its forgotten people.

Use **F** or the touch interaction button to talk, read, turn lenses and carry the flame. Combat uses the existing attack, special ability, target lock and jump controls. The guide and gold map marker lead to the current objective; blue markers show available interactions. The journal keeps recaps of both earlier chapters.

The portal circuit is **City → Pine Islet → Yard → Observatory → City** once the relay is complete. Before then, the yard portal still goes home. The observatory always offers a return passage, so exploring or leaving a fight cannot trap a player. All travel still requires reading and casting from the physical portal book.

Completed milestones, individual memories, memorials and XP save immediately. Unfinished lens arrangements, relay runs and boss fights restart after reloading or leaving the map. Falling resets the active relay or fight. Menus and conversations pause challenge time. Leaving the boss arena also resets the fight; discoveries stay saved. The new dialogue uses the existing text conversation system; new voice recordings are not included.

The observatory and its spectral characters and boss are built from original geometry. They need no additional downloaded models. The previous region’s terrain is released after travel, as with the existing portal maps.

<summary>Puzzle solution and combat timing</summary>

- Faces cycle **SEA → HEARTH → SKY**. Each turn also advances the next lens: root → rain → moon → root.
- Targets: **ROOT = SKY, RAIN = SEA, MOON = HEARTH**. From the starting arrangement, turn root twice and rain once. Three turns undo a move.
- The relay can visit **DUSK, TIDE and DAWN** in any order. Returning to the beacon is required; revisiting it early does not refill the timer.
- The Unwritten warns for **3.2 seconds** before a pulse with a **12-unit radius**. Move outside it or jump more than one unit above the floor. Its heart then opens for **3.4 seconds**.
- At two-thirds and one-third health, it summons two **110-health** guards. Kill both to remove their shield. In phase three the warning shortens to **2.4 seconds**, and the pulse deals more damage.
- Leaving more than **38 units** from the centre resets the boss attempt.
